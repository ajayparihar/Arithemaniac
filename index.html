How to Play Arithamaniac

1

Select & Combine: Click a source tile, then click a target tile to combine them using the selected mode's math operation (e.g. 4 + 3 = 7).

2

Match Targets: Match any of the active Target Numbers shown at the top. Matching a target clears it, awards big score bonuses, and refills your moves!

3

Spawning & Operations: Every valid move combines tiles and spawns a new random number in an empty cell. Use arithmetic wisely!

4

Modes: Switch between Addition (+), Subtraction (−), Multiplication (×), Division (÷), or Mixed (±×) for different challenges.
